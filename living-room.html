ATMOSPHERIC LIGHTING & CEILINGS

A great living room uses light as an invisible material.

We reject harsh direct downlights in favor of a layered lighting scheme: warm 3000K indirect LED coves that gently bounce light off ceilings, magnetic low-voltage track fixtures to highlight artwork, and dimmable mood settings for movie nights.
