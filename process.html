Our Seamless Process

From Design to Delivery

Experience a structured, transparent 6-step journey from initial concept to your turnkey move-in.

Meet Your Designer

Share your ideas and floor plan to receive personalized 3D designs and an instant quote. Our senior architects explore your lifestyle, storage requirements, and aesthetics to craft a bespoke design roadmap.

Book Your Order

Pay 10% of the woodwork and home decor services to book your order. Lock in your dedicated project architect, milestone timeline, and price protection with 100% transparent quotation.

Finalise Your Design

Choose your materials, finishes and colours. Pay the next 10% of woodwork and home decor services to lock designs and begin on-site laser precision masking and quality benchmark checks.

Send Designs to Factory

Pay 30% of woodwork and 80% of home decor services to begin automated factory production. Your customized modular components are cut, routed, and edge-banded with German CNC precision.

Kick Off Dispatch

Pay the remaining 50% of woodwork to dispatch your order post-production and begin installation. All items are multi-layer packed, quality-inspected, and delivered safely to your doorstep.

Move In & Celebrate

Enjoy flawless white-glove installation, a professional post-assembly deep clean, and an exhaustive 50-point quality audit. Receive your certified 10-year warranty document and the keys to your new home!
