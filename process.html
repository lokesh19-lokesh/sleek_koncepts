How We Build Together

THE 9-STEP SLEEK KONCEPTS JOURNEY

We take the unpredictability out of interior construction with clear milestone sign-offs.

01

INITIAL CONSULTATION & DISCOVERY

We meet in person at our Karimnagar studio or via virtual walkthrough to review your floor plan, understand your daily family lifestyle, discuss architectural aesthetics, and outline budget parameters.

Milestone 1
02

SITE MEASUREMENT & LASER AUDIT

Our technical survey team conducts laser-calibrated on-site scans of every wall, ceiling level, beam drops, plumbing lines, and existing electrical junctions to guarantee zero dimensional discrepancies.

Milestone 2
03

SPACE PLANNING & 2D SCHEMATICS

We draft comprehensive 2D floor plans defining circulation traffic, kitchen triangle ergonomics, wardrobe bay divisions, and custom furniture placements for your review and approval.

Milestone 3
04

DESIGN CONCEPT & MOODBOARDS

Curating the cohesive brand language for your home: tactile material combinations, wall finishes, ceiling drops, acoustic paneling styles, and color harmonies.

Milestone 4
05

PHOTOREALISTIC 3D VISUALIZATION

You don't have to imagine your home. We produce high-resolution 3D renders displaying exact finishes, realistic evening lighting, and textures before manufacturing begins.

Milestone 5
06

MATERIAL & HARDWARE SELECTION

Feel and inspect physical samples at our showroom: touch genuine acrylics, PU paints, quartz slabs, fluted louvers, and test German soft-close drawer slides first-hand.

Milestone 6
07

FACTORY PRECISION MANUFACTURING

Approved production drawings are transferred directly to automated CNC routers for millimeter-accurate sizing, grooving, and high-heat PUR edge banding under controlled conditions.

Milestone 7
08

SEAMLESS SITE INSTALLATION

Flat-pack modules arrive securely wrapped at your residence. Our trained installation technicians erect, align, and anchor units cleanly with zero messy sawdust or disruption.

Milestone 8
09

QUALITY AUDIT & FINAL HANDOVER

Our senior project manager conducts a 120-point quality audit, followed by industrial deep cleaning of all cabinets, keys handover, and delivery of your warranty certificate.
