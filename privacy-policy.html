PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: January 2026. How Sleek Koncepts collects, protects, and utilizes client information.

1. Information We Collect

When you schedule a consultation, request an interior estimate, or communicate with us through our website, we may collect personal details including:

  • Your full name and contact telephone / WhatsApp number.
  • Email address.
  • Residential project location, property size, and room configuration.
  • Specific design style preferences and budget indicators.

2. How We Use Your Information

The collected information is used strictly to:

  • Schedule your 1-on-1 space planning and 3D design sessions.
  • Draft accurate preliminary estimates and Bill of Quantities (BOQ).
  • Coordinate site laser audits, material deliveries, and installation schedules.
  • Provide customer support, post-handover warranty assistance, and maintenance notifications.

3. Cookies & Website Analytics

Our website may employ standard browser cookies to understand site performance, user navigation flow, and optimize page load speeds. You can configure your browser to decline cookies, though certain interactive features (like form autofill) may function differently.

4. Data Security

We implement commercial-grade digital security protocols to prevent unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure of your personal data. We never sell, rent, or lease your contact information to third-party telemarketers or advertisers.

5. Third-Party Integrations

Our website integrates third-party services such as Google Maps (for studio navigation) and WhatsApp API (for direct messaging). These platforms operate under their respective privacy policies.

6. User Rights

You possess the right to review the contact information we hold for your project or request deletion of your consultation inquiry record. To exercise this right, simply email us at kondapalkulasairam@gmail.com.
